DRIVER / CLAIRE ERHUANGA

I'm Claire Erhuanga, a multidisciplinary designer with a background in Software Engineering and a strong interest in creating digital experiences that feel intuitive, useful and visually considered.

My work sits across UI/UX design, visual communication, creative technology and digital storytelling. I enjoy understanding how people interact with products, identifying where experiences become unnecessarily difficult and designing clearer ways forward.

Beyond UI/UX, I explore video editing, graphic design, UX writing, logo design and vibe coding, allowing me to approach digital experiences from several creative perspectives.

Outside design, I love racing games — the speed, precision, environments and constant pursuit of a better line. That fascination inspired this portfolio.
